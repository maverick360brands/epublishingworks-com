Crime reporter Lacy Erikson struggles with the accidental death of her husband and the feeling that God betrayed her.

Then an unusual murder puts her in the path of former police offer Michael Lindahl. A gorgeous hunk of a man with a rocky past, Mike rekindles Lacy's desires, showing her a future she never imagined. How can she not love a man who makes his own marshmallows?

But first Mike and Lacy must learn to trust—in each other as well as the Lord.
