Bestselling, award-winning author Judith E. French is the author of 58 novels and the mother of bestselling author Colleen French. Judith is known for her tales of high adventure, historical accuracy, and passionate romance, marked by strong women and the men who captured their hearts. Judith lives with her husband in an 18th century, rural farmhouse that has been in her family for generations.
