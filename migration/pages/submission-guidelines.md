## Partnering with Authors for Success

At ePublishing *Works!*, we believe in empowering authors to achieve their goals without sacrificing their passion for storytelling. Whether you're breathing new life into your backlist or looking for the right partner for your latest manuscript, we're here to help you focus on what matters most: your writing.

**We're actively seeking *backlist* (previously published) and *frontlist* (not yet published) titles right now!**

Career-minded authors who have a minimum of three completed books (backlist and/or frontlist) in the same genre or sub-genre are best. If this sounds like you, we'd love to hear from you.

It's okay if some of your backlist titles are still in print as physical copies—our expertise can help you bring them into the digital age.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### How do I submit my manuscript?

Pretty easy peasy. [Please submit your manuscript by filling out our submission form](/submit-your-manuscript/).

#### Do you accept backlist titles?

Yes, if rights have been reverted from the original publisher. Acceptance of any book is subject to review by our experienced publishing team.

We are happy to guide our authors through what that looks like.

#### Will you edit my manuscript for me?

We will proofread but we have limited capacity to edit manuscripts. As one of our authors, we can connect you with the right people – legends who can help you.

#### Does my book fit your requirements?

Great question. We'll review your manuscript to make certain.

In general, we're looking for a commercially viable writing style that meets the expectations of the genre. We prefer “evergreen” books. Serial titles with 3+ titles are preferred.

#### What genres do you publish?

We primarily publish crime fiction, mystery, cozy mystery, and a lot of historical romance.

Yes, we will consider other genres, although we do **not** publish erotica.

For Christian fiction and Christian non-fiction, please see our sister brand, [RiseUpPublications.com](http://www.riseuppublications.com/)

#### Do you accept first-time books from a new author?

Yes, of course! We'll look for evidence of quality and a plan for 6+ titles. Trust us, you will want to write a series too.

**Still have questions?** No problem, [reach out to us here](/contact/).
