Kim Crosier is being blackmailed. Desperate and scared, she turns to her friend Christy for help. Together, they concoct a risky plan to turn the tables on the blackmailer. But when things go awry, and the blackmailer is found dead, both Kim and Christy become prime suspects.

With a crucial Child Services hearing looming, Christy cannot afford to be entangled in a murder investigation. She and Quinn must race against time to clear their names and uncover the truth behind the blackmailer's death. As they dig deeper, they unearth a web of deceit and a trail of victims, each with a motive to kill.

Join Christy, Quinn, and Stormy the Cat, as they navigate a labyrinth of lies and uncover secrets that could ruin reputations and lives. Will they find the true killer before it's too late?
