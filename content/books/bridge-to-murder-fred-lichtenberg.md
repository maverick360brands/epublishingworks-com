The shocking disappearance of a teenager under the Whitestone Bridge brings shivers to a fragile community. Two years earlier, a young woman was murdered in the same area. The killer has never been caught.

Hank Reed takes on his most challenging and personal case when the sister of his childhood friend, who had been missing more than twenty-five years, receives an anonymous letter claiming her brother is dead.

Hoax? Maybe, but she hires Hank to investigate, and as he plunges into the cold case, he quickly realizes there is more to the story than just a missing person. His findings will rock the town of Whitestone forever.
