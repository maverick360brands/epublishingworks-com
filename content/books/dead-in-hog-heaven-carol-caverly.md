Thea Barlow has quit her Chicago job and returned to Wyoming—and her fiancé Max Holman—to work as a freelance writer. In Hog Heaven to investigate the ruins of an old rural whorehouse, Thea stumbles upon a woman's body and is fingered for murder. Now, Thea and Max must ferret out Hog Heaven's secrets and expose the person attempting to frame Thea before she and Max become the next victims... if the town's mean-spirited chicken doesn't get them first.
