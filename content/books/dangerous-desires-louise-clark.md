Indebted to Nicholas Prescott, Earl of Wroxton, for her very survival, young Stephanie de la Riviére is miserable, homesick and determined to do whatever it takes—from selling the family jewels to masquerading as a highwayman—to again see her beloved father, an aristocrat caught up in the dangerous French Revolution.

Nicholas is determined to prevent the exasperated Stephanie from putting herself in harm's way. But keeping the beautiful brunette out of outrageous scenarios isn't why Nicholas remains awake at night. It's his own desire to match his ward's fiery spirit to his burning passion that won't let him rest.
