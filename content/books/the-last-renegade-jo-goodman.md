Bitter Springs, Wyoming — 1888

Kellen Coltrane arrives in Bitter Springs carrying a stranger's Colts—and a story Raine Berry never expected to hear. The widow who owns the Pennyroyal Hotel hired the legendary "Nat Church" to protect her town from the powerful Burdick family. Instead, she's met by a man who claims Church was murdered on the way.

Bitter Springs needs a defender. Jurors from the Burdick trial are turning up dead, the marshal has been killed, and a corrupt new lawman ensures justice won't come from the badge. Reluctantly, Kellen steps into the role he never asked for, determined to honor a dying man's promise.

Raine doesn't trust him—but she can't ignore the safety he brings… or the dangerous pull growing between them. When a marriage of convenience becomes the only way to keep her safe, the lines blur between necessity and desire.

As threats close in, Kellen and Raine must stand together—before the Burdicks' revenge destroys everything, including their fragile new love.
