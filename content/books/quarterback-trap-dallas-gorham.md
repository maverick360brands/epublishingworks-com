Graciela Perez, gorgeous fiancée of quarterback Bob Martinez, disappears one week before the Super Bowl. Martinez hires Carlos McCrary to find her but won't let McCrary involve the police.

McCrary soon discovers Graciela was kidnapped by a mob boss and suspects a connection to a recent 100 million-dollar wager made on the game.

As McCrary untangles a web of lies and corruption, he discovers both Graciela and Martinez have dark secrets…secrets that could get them killed—including McCrary.
