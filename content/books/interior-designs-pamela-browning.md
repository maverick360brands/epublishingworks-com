Workaholic Cathryn Mulqueen has achieved everything she wanted—a successful design studio, friends who matter, and local celebritydom. But something is missing from her life. At her high-school reunion, Drew Sedgwick—a boy she hardly noticed when a teen—understands. Standing at a crossroads,

Drew, now a charismatic, successful man and divorced father with a seven-year-old daughter, wants more—including Cathryn. Drew's passionate kisses on the beach convince Cathryn they're a perfect match, but Cathryn must decide how much she's willing to give up to make the three of them a family.
