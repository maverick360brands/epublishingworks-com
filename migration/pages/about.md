## Created by authors for authors,<br>because ePublishing *Works!*

***ePublishingWorks!* was born out of a shared vision: to create a publishing house that puts authors first.**

Founded in 2011 by two national bestselling authors and a passionate entrepreneur with a love for books, we set out to change the way indie publishing works—starting with empowering writers to focus on what they do best: writing.

![](/images/site/author-writing-book.jpg) <!-- stock photo: smiling man in glasses writing in a notebook at a café table (no alt text on the live site) -->

#### What began as a bold idea quickly grew into a thriving community.

**By 2013, we tripled in size, becoming one of the most trusted and sought-after names in next-generation indie publishing.**

**Since then, we've achieved milestones that speak to the success of our authors.**

In 2019 alone, we sold over 1 million books, landed three titles on the NYT/USA Today Bestsellers lists, and helped numerous authors reach the Top 100 charts on platforms like Amazon, Barnes & Noble, iBooks, and Kobo. And we're just getting started.

#### We do more than publish books.<br>We champion authors.

**Our approach is simple yet revolutionary:** we prioritize your success by offering higher royalties, taking the weight of promotion off your shoulders, and supporting you every step of the way. Whether it's crafting strategies to increase your sales or amplifying your voice to reach new readers, we're here to help you thrive in a competitive industry.

If you're a career-driven author looking for more than just a publishing partner—if you want representation that values your work and your vision—you've found the right place. At ePublishing Works!, your stories are our purpose, and your success is our mission.
